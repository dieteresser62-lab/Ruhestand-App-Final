# Data licensing and source attribution

## Deutsche Bundesbank / ESCB statistics

Source: Deutsche Bundesbank.

The pinned long-series PDF and the derived table extract are used under the
policy regarding the reuse of ESCB statistics:

https://www.bundesbank.de/en/homepage/user-information/terms-of-use-regarding-the-reuse-of-escb-statistics-621188

The source is quoted and the published observations are retained without
modification. The app's separately described transformation interprets the
published annual-average rate as a gross annual cash-return proxy. Bundesbank
and ECB disclaimers, revision risk and continuity limits remain applicable.

For the Euro Short-Term Rate (`EURSTR`), the pinned Bundesbank source requires
the European Central Bank administrator disclaimer and states that it also
applies to Bundesbank publications:

https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/financial_markets_and_interest_rates/euro_short-term_rate/html/index.en.html
