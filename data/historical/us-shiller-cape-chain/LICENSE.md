# Source usage boundary

The source workbook is distributed from Robert J. Shiller's public data page.
No explicit open redistribution licence was located for the workbook during
Slice 06. The publisher/Yale disclaimer applies.

source hash. The absence of an explicit open licence must not be represented as
an open-data grant or as external validation of the generated series. These
source terms are separate from the RuhestandsApp project licence.
